# AIDEMOCRACY UK and Canada Data Centre Planning Analysis

In recent years ‘hyperscaling’, the idea that the best way to improve the efficacy of Artificial Intelligence models is to scale their computing architecture, has ascended among  US-led ‘Big Tech’. The exponential growth of compute implied in this definition demands a similarly exponential construction project. In practice, this has meant the sudden proliferation of a new kind of infrastructure: the hyperscale data centre.

This work is part of the 3-year international project AIDEMOCRACY within the Data Justice Lab, funded by the TransAtlantic Platform scheme (ESRC), and contributes to an emerging critical literature on hyperscale development by mapping the UK's and Canada’s data centre build-outs as they are made visible by these countries' planning systems.
